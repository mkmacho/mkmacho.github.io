\section{Research profile}

Applied economist and computational researcher with graduate training in economics, mathematics, and computer science. My proposed doctoral research studies how infrastructure-finance arrangements distribute fiscal and foreign-exchange risk and decision-making authority in lower-income economies, beginning with Malawi's energy sector. Previous work at the Ellison Institute of Technology, OECD, NBER, FAIR at the Norwegian School of Economics, and the World Inequality Lab spans quantitative modelling, policy evaluation, experiments, and large-scale data construction.
