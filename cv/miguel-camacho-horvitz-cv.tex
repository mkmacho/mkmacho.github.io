\section{Research profile}

Research economist and computational researcher with graduate training in economics, mathematics, and computer science. My proposed doctoral research studies how currency hierarchy, capital flows, and financing institutions distribute risk, investment authority, productive capability, and inequality across North--South relations, starting with infrastructure finance in Malawi's energy sector. Previous work at the Ellison Institute of Technology, OECD, NBER, FAIR at the Norwegian School of Economics, and the World Inequality Lab spans quantitative modelling, policy evaluation, survey experiments, and large-scale data construction.
